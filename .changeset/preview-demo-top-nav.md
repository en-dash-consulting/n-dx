Demo page shell: the three stages (Analysis, Plan, Work) are tabs in a top bar beside the logo and project info. The bottom bar keeps the server status, a settings cog and a Commands button — Commands lifts a sheet over the current page with the runner and the full reference; the cog opens the settings pages as a full overlay with an ✕ to close. Each stage page carries two small vertical buttons on its edges that step around the loop (Analysis → Plan → Work → Analysis). The static exporter bakes the sheet and settings pages into their own hosts.
