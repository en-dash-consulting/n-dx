The dashboard's Work page splits Ready to run into your tasks and the unassigned ones grouped by product area, and adds a Statistics section with the PRD's numbers.

Statistics holds the totals, overall progress, status breakdown, priority distribution and structure health; change progress stays off Work. Ready to run counts the tasks assigned to someone else rather than listing them. The Audit view is now a tile on Work's Tune mode, and the Rex dashboard's quick actions are a PRD tools side panel.
