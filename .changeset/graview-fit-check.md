`ndx graview check` holds the emitted snapshot to the declaration, not only the declaration: every record's kind, fields, enum values, number ranges and required fields, and every edge's relation, target kind and cardinality. `emit` warns about a record that does not fit and `check` fails on it, naming the record, instead of the misfit surfacing when the face loads. On n-dx it found runs, commits and blockers that name a v1 feature or epic the proposed product layer turned into a capability or area; `ranFor`, `landedFor` and `blockedBy` now admit those.
