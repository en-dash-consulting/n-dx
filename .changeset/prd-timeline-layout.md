The PRD can be laid out as a timeline: Tune's Shipped tile shows it, and the Tasks page has a Tree | Timeline switch.

The timeline lays completed items along a horizontal day or week axis, with now at the right edge and older work further to the left. Each completion hangs off the axis on a stem that turns diagonal and carries the item's title, which opens the item. A busy day or week collapses its overflow into a "+N more" list. The top-level items still open are listed to the right of today, and a month bar above the graph jumps to the start of each month. Shipped now spans Tune's full width at the foot of the page, its "Open" still going to the Merge Graph; on the Tasks page the tree stays the default and the choice is remembered per browser.
