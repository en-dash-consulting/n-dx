The Graview declaration n-dx emits now carries a design, as data: a glance and page groups on every kind, computed fields (open changes and zones per capability, open tasks, runs and tokens per change, components per zone), a brand from one accent with a hue per kind and the n-dx mark, a home written as blocks, card, row and page blocks for capabilities, changes, zones and runs, and seven titled places: changes by status, runs by outcome, where capabilities live in code, what binds what, when changes closed, needs attention, and spend. `graview check` is clean on it.

`graview.app` in the project config names a Graview product built from the emitted document (the sibling `n-dx-graview` repository: a shell, home and record pages in n-dx's own words over the derived ones). With it set, `ndx graview serve .` emits and runs that product's dev server on the fresh projection, with `NDX_GRAVIEW_DIR` pointing at the files, instead of the bare `graview serve`, which is the store's wire and draws nothing.
