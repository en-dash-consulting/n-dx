Graview 0.1.22 answered every ask the n-dx run filed, so both packages take it and drop the workarounds that stood in for it. The adapter writes the snapshot in Graview's line format (format 2: a header saying when it was made and what it holds, then a record a line), by its own writer held byte for byte to `@graview/core/snapshot`'s, and `emitProjection` takes `generatedAt`. The declaration names `$schema` for editor validation, declares `fieldRoles` on run and commit, a document-level fact `mapProposed` that the home reads with `fact()`, a `key` on every titled block the face claims, each kind's own wording of `under`, and sorts a change's tasks by `choices`. The hand-written document types give way to the format Graview publishes.

The face pins `@graview/*` and `graview` at 0.1.22 and `pnpm doctor` holds them together. Its sync is `SyncEngine` from `@graview/core/sync` with `conflicts: "theirs"`, a `version` field on each synced resource and read-only resources with a `because` sentence, guarding the store; the hand pre-empt, the seeded links and the refusal table are gone. The dev door is `mcpDoor` over `mcpClient`, the page reaches it with `doorClient`, the remote is `mcpRemote`, and the snapshot is read with `fetchSnapshot` by byte range, two kinds at a time. Declared blocks resolve with `resolveFor` and are claimed by key; the store attributes acts to `rememberedPrincipal()`; the shell keeps the bar without its places; types are generated from the document with `graview types` and the models read through `typedReader`. `shots.mjs`, the fixture-drift test, the version-pin test and the fixture copy of the document are deleted in favour of `graview shots`, `graview doctor` and `compileDocumentFiles`.
