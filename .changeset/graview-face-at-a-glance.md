The Graview face says what is true at a glance. The Inbox, "Needs attention", the Changes list's Inbox filter and a change's badge count only open changes, not finished work the rules never placed (on n-dx, 15 instead of 142), and the Inbox page lists the finished ones apart. The home lists work under way (in progress, blocked, failing) apart from work waiting to start, instead of an always-empty "Changing now". Area cards show how many of their capabilities are met and how many open changes sit on the area or anything under it, instead of the slug and the proposed flag. A zone page shows Components only when the zone has some. The "Ask" field no longer covers the last rows of a page.
