Placing an Inbox change now previews what Place will do and why, and its button names the action, such as "Touch Refunds" or "Amend Refunds".

Once a target is chosen, the placement section on the change's detail panel, the Inbox and placement mode draws the change, the relation and the target. Touches is a dashed edge and Amends a solid heavy one. The preview shows rex's strength and reasons for a shortlist target, rex's rule for its relation, or that the pick was the person's own or the model's. A before/after shows whether the target's statement and criteria stay as they are or how an amendment changes them. When another open change already amends the same target, the Amends preview names it and warns that the first to apply wins and the other may be refused. It redraws as the target, relation or amendment changes, and nothing is written until the button is pressed.

A reparent proposal in the Ask panel shows the same picture: the item, the parent it leaves, the parent it joins and the rationale. Its accept button reads "Move to <parent>".
