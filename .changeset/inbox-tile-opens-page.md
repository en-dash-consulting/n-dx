The Inbox tile on Plan is now clickable: its **Place** buttons work in place, and its header opens the Inbox page instead of a full-screen preview.

Other tiles stay previews until opened, so their contents take no clicks. A row's **Place** now opens the Inbox page with that change selected, where its shortlist, relation and **Place & next** are. Placement mode on the map stays one click away through **Place on the map ↗**.
