The Sidecar Explain lens now explains a product area, a capability or a
sourcevision zone. A capability's packet lists each capability criterion as
`met`, `not met` or `not verified` with its evidence, an area's carries its
capabilities, a zone's carries the analysis summary, and on a v1 tree the packet
says there is no product layer. The lens instructions keep "capability criteria"
apart from "done when".
