The dashboard's Plan page can show only your work, focus on one stage, plan an Inbox item by dropping it on a product area, switch Solo / Team, and give a short tour.

For me / Everyone and the stage chips sit over the product areas and also narrow the Inbox panel. Each Inbox row offers "Plan in <area>" for its best guess, and a row dragged onto an area is placed there (a drop on an area with no candidate opens placement mode). Solo hides assignees and For me / Everyone; both choices are remembered in the browser. "Take the tour" walks through the page in four steps.
