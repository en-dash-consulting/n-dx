The migration plan's present-tense statement check accepts a statement that names "the task", "the change", "the item" or "the PR", or opens with a code span such as "`ndx self-heal` persists …". Those are product vocabulary in a PRD tool; only "this feature/task/change/…" still marks work. Capabilities whose model statement was rejected no longer fall back to the template statement.
