LS12: Terraform becomes a language pack whose detail will extend Sterling H's infrastructure.json (analyzers/infrastructure.ts, which today extracts resource blocks with a regex and maps types to coarse kinds via IAC_KINDS). In this PR the Terraform model stays in memory: nothing is written to infrastructure.json until the conversation with Sterling H. infrastructure.ts and IAC_KINDS are not edited.
