Sourcevision writes readiness.json, sdlc-profile.json and infrastructure.json, and GET /api/status already serves sv.readiness and an sv.infrastructure count (packages/web/src/server/routes-status.ts). No viewer or hub UI renders any of it: the Overview has no section for CI/CD readiness or discovered infrastructure, the hub cards stop at repoName/remoteHost, and the iso-map hero is the only place infrastructure appears. This feature makes the outside infrastructure and pipeline maturity visible and usable.
