Add a visual (a stepper or flow strip, not prose) that shows the steps (capture → place → ready → work → apply), highlights the step the project currently needs most (e.g. Inbox items waiting for placement), and says in one line what to do at that step. Each step links to the tile or action where it is done. It complements, and does not replace, the existing lifecycle strip of counts."
