description: "Cascade `sv analyze` spawns a detached `sv narrate` and records it in `manifest.narration`. Before this task nothing outside sourcevision read that field, and a superseded narration was simply lost.\n\nObserved on n-site2 (2026-09-22): analyze at 18:07:32 spawned narrate at 18:07:48, and its naming phase wrote 18 renames. A second analyze finished at 18:08:28. At 18:08:58 narrate dropped the descriptions/findings for four zones as \"superseded by a newer analysis\", and nothing re-queued them.\n\n**Built:**\n1. `takeOverNarration` (sv `narrate.ts`), called at the start of `cmdAnalyze`. A live narrator is stopped with SIGTERM and recorded as `failed: superseded`. A pending narration with a dead pid is recorded as `failed: narrator exited without finishing`. The zones and names it had not finished are returned. Failed narrations are retried only for superseded, exited or could-not-spawn reasons.\n2. `carryNarration` folds those into this run's pending sets, keeping only ids that still exist in zones.json, and prints a `[narrate]` line with carried and dropped counts. A `--fast` run leaves them recorded for the next full run.\n3. `sv narrate` clears `names` once its naming phase has written, so a takeover never re-queues names that are already done.\n4. `ndx status` prints one narration line from `packages/core/narration-status.js` when narration is pending, exited or failed.\n5. The dashboard overview header shows the same notice (`viewer/views/narration-notice.ts`).\n6. `ndx plan` passes `--wait` to `sv analyze`.\n\n**Not built:** carried ids are matched by id only, not also by file-set overlap. `ndx refresh` is unchanged, because the narrator regenerates the outputs itself when it finishes."
