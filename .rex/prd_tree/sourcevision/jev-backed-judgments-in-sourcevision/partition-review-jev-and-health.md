Diagnosis, measured offline against n-site2's own inventory/imports with the current build:\n\n- A fresh partition from the current code has **0** zones of ≤ 2 files. The fragments were not produced by today's algorithm.\n- With the stored zones.json as `previousZones`, `analyzeZones` **reuses** it verbatim: `inputFingerprint` matched (`0f8250f28bf1c072`), so the bad partition was frozen.\n- With the fingerprint forced to differ, the **stability bias** (previous assignment seeds `louvainPhase1` and adds co-zone edges) rebuilt the same 20 fragments. So neither a file change nor a `ZONE_ALGORITHM_VERSION` bump would have escaped it; only deleting `.sourcevision/` would.\n- The originally suspected cause — `mergeSmallCommunities` skipping edge-less small communities — does not produce these fragments on n-site2 and was not changed.\n\n**Built** (`analyzers/partition-review.ts`, wired in `analyzeZones`):\n1. `assessPartitionHealth`: zones, small zones (≤ 2 files), numeric-suffix ids, largest share → verdict `healthy | borderline | fragmented` (≥ 8 zones; ≥ 40% small = fragmented; ≥ 20% small or ≥ 25% numeric ids = borderline).\n2. Borderline → one Jev Noul over the whole map (file groupings, metrics, crossing degree; no names or ids), `zone.judge` task class, cached like every judgment; ≤ 0.3 rejects.\n3. A rejected previous partition is neither reused nor used as the stability seed; identity preservation after a rejection needs ≥ 0.8 overlap (0.5 otherwise), so fragments' ids/names do not leak onto merged zones.\n4. At most once per `inputFingerprint`: the review is stored as `zones.partitionReview` and carried; a rejected-for-this-fingerprint review is trusted on the next run.\n5. `manifest.lastAnalysis.partition` and one `[partition]` CLI line; documented in docs/guide/configuration.md.\n\n**Not built, left for follow-up:** a per-small-zone Jev Choice (`keep | merge-into:<candidate>`) that merges without a full re-partition; and absorbing edge-less small communities by directory in `mergeSmallCommunities`. The fresh n-site2 partition still has weak spots the health signals do not flag — a 599-file component zone and a 250-file `utils` grab-bag — which need a different signal than fragment share."
